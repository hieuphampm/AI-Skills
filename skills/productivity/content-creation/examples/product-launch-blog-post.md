# Content Draft

## Meta

- **Slug**: `instant-reports-automated-reporting`
- **Audience**: Product managers and ops leads who build reports manually.
- **Goal**: Get readers to try the Instant Reports feature.

## Hook
Building a weekly report used to take your team hours of copy-pasting and formatting. Now it takes one click.

## Body
### What Instant Reports does
Instant Reports turns your existing data into a clean, shareable report automatically. Pick a template, choose your data source, and it handles the rest — no spreadsheets, no manual formatting.

### Who it's for
If you're a product manager or ops lead who assembles reports by hand every week, Instant Reports removes that repetitive work. You keep control over what's included; the tool handles the assembly.

### How to get started
1. Open the Reports tab.
2. Choose a template.
3. Connect your data source and click Generate.

## Call to Action
Try Instant Reports free for 14 days — set up your first report in under five minutes.
