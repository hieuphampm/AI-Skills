# Content Style Guide

Consult this reference during Steps 2–5 of `content-creation`.

## 1. Structure
- **Hook**: Open with a question, surprising fact, or the reader's pain point in the first 1–2 sentences.
- **Headings**: Use a clear H1 title and H2/H3 sections that a skimmer can follow top-to-bottom.
- **One idea per section**: Keep each section focused; split if a section covers two ideas.
- **Call-to-action (CTA)**: End with a single, specific next step (subscribe, try the tool, read the doc).

## 2. Audience & Tone
- **Match reading level** to the audience (e.g., plain language for general readers, precise terms for technical readers).
- **Consistent voice**: Pick one tone (friendly, authoritative, neutral) and hold it throughout.
- **Speak to "you"**: Address the reader directly; lead with their benefit, not the product's features.

## 3. SEO
- **Title**: Include the primary keyword near the front; keep it ~50–60 characters.
- **Meta description**: 140–160 characters, includes the keyword, and states the value of reading.
- **Keyword placement**: Primary keyword in the title, first paragraph, and one H2; use natural variations, never keyword-stuff.
- **Slug**: Short, hyphenated, keyword-based (e.g., `how-to-write-a-blog-hook`).

## 4. Readability & Editing
- **Short sentences**: Aim for 15–20 words on average; break up long ones.
- **Active voice**: Prefer "We shipped the feature" over "The feature was shipped."
- **Scannable**: Use bullets and bold for key points; avoid walls of text.
- **Cut filler**: Remove "very", "really", "in order to", and redundant phrases.
- **Proofread**: Check spelling, grammar, and consistent terminology before delivery.
