# Analysis Framework

Consult this reference during Steps 3–5 of `market-analysis`.

## 1. Fundamental Analysis
- **Valuation**: P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA, P/S — compare against the company's history and sector peers.
- **Financial health**: revenue growth, gross/operating margins, free cash flow, debt-to-equity.
- **Competitive position**: market share, moat (switching costs, network effects, brand), management quality.
- **Quality check**: flag any figure that is stale, unaudited, or from a low-credibility source.

## 2. Technical Analysis
- **Trend**: higher highs/higher lows (uptrend) or lower highs/lower lows (downtrend); note the timeframe.
- **Support / resistance**: identify the nearest key levels from price history.
- **Momentum**: relative strength, volume trend, and any notable divergence.
- **Caveat**: technicals describe price behavior, not intrinsic value; present them as context, not prediction.

## 3. Risk Assessment
- **Company-specific**: product concentration, regulatory exposure, key-person risk.
- **Sector**: cyclicality, competitive dynamics, input costs.
- **Macro**: interest rates, inflation, currency, geopolitical factors.
- **Catalysts**: upcoming earnings, product launches, policy decisions.
- **Confidence level**: rate the overall analysis as High / Medium / Low based on data quality and recency.

## 4. Sourcing & Honesty
- Record the **source** and **as-of date** for every material figure.
- If a number cannot be verified, mark it as a **placeholder to verify** and lower confidence.
- Separate **facts** (sourced) from **interpretation** (your analysis) in the report.
